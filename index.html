About BCPASS

Built for the business,
not just the bank.

BCPASS started from a simple observation: when a growing company needs to open accounts or build relationships with several banks or financial institutions, it ends up doing the same KYC exercise over and over — the same documents, the same questions, the same explanations, once per institution.

We built BCPASS to make that a one-time exercise. Our AI helps you build a verified, structured profile of your business once — then you decide who sees what, and when.

We work with corporates and SMEs who are opening new banking relationships, raising financing, or simply trying to keep their own company records organized and current. Behind the Pass you share is a platform we hope your business keeps coming back to on its own terms — a place to see your own structure clearly, keep your profile current, and better understand your own financial position, not just something you open when a bank asks for it.

What we believe

01

You should only tell your story once

Repeating the same KYC exercise for every institution wastes your time and theirs. One verified profile should travel with you.

02

Sharing isn't skipping

A Pass gives a bank, partner, or institution a verified head start — it's never a substitute for their own checks, and we don't build it to be one.

03

AI should do the busywork, not the judgment calls

Our AI structures, extracts, and flags — it never guesses a fact you haven't told it or a document hasn't shown. You stay the authority on your own business.

How AI is embedded

AI does the paperwork.
You stay the authority.

We use AI throughout BCPASS — always in service of getting your business represented accurately, never to replace your own review of it.

Conversation

Turning what you say into structure

When you describe your business in your own words, AI organizes what you say into a structured profile, asking follow-up questions where something's missing.

Documents

Reading what you upload

AI works out what each document is and pulls out the relevant detail, so you don't have to label or retype anything yourself.

Diagram

Mapping your structure

AI turns your ownership, directors, suppliers, and customers into a diagram, connecting the pieces so it's easy for anyone to follow.

Review

Checking for gaps, not guessing

Our AI is built to flag what looks inconsistent or incomplete and ask you about it — never to invent a detail you haven't told us or a document hasn't shown.

Context

Adding context, always sourced

Where it's useful, AI adds general market or industry context — always clearly labeled as research, never presented as a fact about your business.

You

You review everything

Every AI-generated summary, diagram, and flag is something you — and whoever you share it with — can review before it's relied on. AI drafts; you decide.

Our standing rule

Never guess, never invent. If our AI isn't sure about something, it says so — it doesn't fill the gap with a detail that merely sounds right.

Privacy

You decide what's
shared, and with whom.

Here's how BCPASS is designed to handle your information.

Your Pass, your control

  • Nothing is shared until you choose to share it — building your profile doesn't send anything to anyone
  • Your Pass has five levels of detail; you choose the level for each bank, partner, or institution you share with
  • One link and QR code per company — raise or lower the level, set an expiry, or turn it off, any time
  • You can see exactly what a recipient will see before you share it

Sharing isn't skipping

  • Every bank, partner, or institution you share a Pass with still runs its own checks — a Pass gives them a verified starting point, not a shortcut around their own process
  • Full company name, documents, and director details stay hidden until a recipient accepts your interest
  • Any AI-added research or context is clearly labeled as such, never presented as a fact about your business or your counterparties

Your account is protected

  • Two-factor authentication is required on every account
  • One login can hold profiles for several companies
  • Each colleague signs in separately — nothing is shared between accounts by default

What we ask for, and why

  • We ask for the information a bank or partner would typically need for KYC — nothing beyond that
  • Documents you upload are used to build and verify your profile, not for any other purpose
  • You can pause, revise, or delete a company profile from your dashboard

This page describes how BCPASS is designed to work today, during our UK & Hong Kong pilot. It's a plain-language description of our approach, not a substitute for formal legal terms — we'll publish those as we move toward general availability.
